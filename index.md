# Dynamsoft Camera Enhancer Mobile Documentation

`Dynamsoft Camera Enhancer (DCE)` handles camera control and video frame acquisition in mobile apps. It opens and configures the device camera, shows the preview in a camera view, and supplies frames to Dynamsoft Barcode Reader, Label Recognizer and Document Normalizer. Its frame filtering and enhanced focus features help those products read barcodes, text and documents more accurately. For an overview of every feature, see the [Introduction]({{ site.introduction }}).
